Systematic trading strategies, backtested properly.

Each article takes one well-known strategy and gives it the treatment it rarely gets: the exact rules, a full metric sheet against a benchmark, and the robustness tests that decide whether the result means anything — parameter sensitivity, in-sample versus out-of-sample, and walk-forward. Every backtest runs on the same open-source engine and free data, and every result file is committed to GitHub, so you can reproduce or refute any number here.
